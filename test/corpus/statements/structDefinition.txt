empty struct

	typename: (typeIdentifier)
	body: (structBodyDefinition)))

================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

	typename: (typeIdentifier)
	body: (structBodyDefinition
		(structFieldDefinition

			type: (typeIdentifier)))))

	typename: (typeIdentifier)
	body: (structBodyDefinition
		(structFieldDefinition

			type: (typeIdentifier)
			default: (defaultDefinition (literalInteger))))))
